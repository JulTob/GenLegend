## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

The sheet's left rail is a **Chip**: symbol, label, value (`AtlasVenustas.Scriba.Chip`). Training maps and `FeaturesKit.Feature` still declare and store that rail as positional tuples `(label, value)` or `(label, value, symbol)`.

The six maps restored on 2026-08-31 (Artificer, Bard, Monk, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard) now declare `Chip(...)`. The older restored maps and other grant sites still use tuples.

## 🧾 Evidence

Tuple `chips=` still in (live files, ignore Finder ` 2.py`):

- `AtlasLusoris/AtlasOfTraining/Map_of_{Barbarian,Cleric,Druid,Fighter,Paladin,Ranger,Rogue}_Training.py`
- `AtlasLusoris/TrainingKit.py` type hint and self-test still index `chip[0]` / `chip[1]`
- Species presentation and other `grant(..., chips=)` call sites that pass tuples

`app/components/character_sheet.py` `_normalize_chip_pairs` historically only understood dicts and sequences.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

New grants declare `Chip(symbol, label, value)` (value may be a callable). Remaining tuple declarations are converted, then the Feature getter can stop emitting tuples. One rail model: Venustas Chip.

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer

Do not merge tuple and Chip as two designs. Tuples are the old packing; Chip is the named one. `FeaturesKit.Feature.chips` already accepts Chip (duck-typed) and still *returns* tuples when the stored form was a tuple, so TrainingKit's self-test stays green until this sweep finishes.

Stay off the duplicate `Grant_Resistance` pair (QST-0081.1) while touching FeaturesKit.

# 🥋 Monk: the Exempt, read against the whole setting

> 📖 **In flow.** 2 of 10 chapters are still proposals. 📜 1 · 📚 2 · 📔 5 · 📖 2

*Mythos analysis, 2026-09-08. Design and literary criticism, not page text. Builds
on Dialog 0018 (core fantasy settled as the shonen ninja: training arcs, named
techniques, speed as freedom, the body as the one weapon nobody can take). This
page does not re-litigate that. It asks what the Monk becomes inside this
setting's peoples, backgrounds and metaphysics, flags what is singular and what
is stock, and drafts the feature lines the sheet lacks entirely. Nothing here is
landed.*

**Where the text lives.** Class and Warrior paragraphs: *nowhere yet*. Dialog
0018's five texts are provisional and unwired; `AtlasOfGuilds/MonkKit.py` is four
bare `Make_Specialization` calls. Lessons: `AtlasOfTraining/Map_of_Monk_Training.py`
(rules only, no inspiration lines). A 2014-era blurb still leaks onto the sheet
from `Map_of_Classes/Training/Monk.py:215` ("Warriors of Mercy manipulate the life
force of others… faceless bringers of life and death"). Culture keys: the Monk
alone among Guilds adds `ninja` (society) and `anime` (legend) to any species.

## 📚 1. What a Monk player is handed today

A level 11 Aasimar Monk of Mercy (seed 3) receives: the Aasimar entry, the
Gambler, thirty rules paragraphs, and one italic sentence from 2014 in the third
person. No class fantasy, no Warrior fantasy, no line above any feature. By the
backgrounds' standard the Monk is the least-inspired sheet on the roster, and it
is the class the author seeded most precisely.

⚠️ **This is the widest gap between designed and shipped.** Dialog 0018's texts
are good (see §7) and have sat provisional since 2026-08-31. The two rules-text
findings it flagged are still live: *"harness your psionic energy"* (Monk's
Focus, Shadow Arts) and the em-dash in Implements of Mercy.

## 📜 2. The name is the sangha's, and the class does not want it

*Monachos*: the solitary one. A word from the contemplative orders, and in this
setting the contemplative order is a **Celestial marker** (`sangha`). So "Monk"
is the second class name on the roster that comes out of the Celestials'
vocabulary: Athens says *barbaros*, the sangha says *monk*. The pensive kind
name everyone.

The real-world precedent explains the mismatch and should stay off the page:
Shaolin is a Buddhist monastery that produced a martial art, so the first
fighting artists outsiders met walked out of temples and were called by the
temple's word. In-world, the art was *seen* in the sangha's houses, not born
there.

✅ **The Barbarian reclaims its name; the Monk should ignore its.** Two
exonyms, two temperaments. The chant says "Barbarians we are". The Monk's
proposed text already has the right attitude toward being named by others:
*"Let them tell stories. Stories travel ahead of you and fight half the fight
before you arrive."* Do not add a reclaiming line to the Monk. Indifference to
the label is the class's character, and it keeps the device from becoming a
house tic.

✅ **The Aasimar Monk is the one Monk for whom the word is literally true**, and
the pairing is not a departure from the spark but the spark embodied. An
Aasimar so in tune with the mortal body that they take it as sacred transcends
its limits through it, merging the mortal and the celestial and channelling the
soul through the fists. The setting already holds the well: the aureola *is*
the crown centre made visible, the thousand-petalled lotus drawn above the head,
and `sangha` reaches `india` at two. **The Aasimar Monk's Focus flows from the
aureola down, through the body, into the fist.** One species-keyed line carries
it, on Monk's Focus, for Aasimar only:

> *It comes down from the ring above your head and out through your hands. The
> body was always the instrument. You only had to consecrate it.*

## 📔 3. Five rings, four Warriors and a capstone

Dialog 0018 read the Warriors through Naruto, Shaolin, Destreza and Avatar. The
Japanese well the `ninja` key reaches (japan 2) holds an older structure that
fits the roster as a set.

Musashi's *Go Rin no Sho* is written in five books named for elements, and
each is a doctrine, not a style:

| Book | Doctrine (Musashi) | Monk | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Earth** | The fundamentals; the ground everything stands on; "the Way is in training" | **Open Hand** | The oldest school and the plainest. True distance, true tempo, no flame and no venom. |
| **Water** | Adaptability; the mind that takes any shape; fluid, exact | **Mercy** | The same hand as balm or blade, decided at the moment. Water is the healer's book. |
| **Fire** | Combat itself; timing, decisiveness, the big release | **Elements** | The forms end in fire, frost and storm. Fire is Musashi's book of the fight, and Elements is the Warrior whose forms are all release. |
| **Wind** | Other schools; knowing what the others do so as not to be caught by it | **Shadow** | The sibling art that trains at night. Wind is the book about what is *not* you, and Shadow is the Warrior that exists only where it allows the fight to. |
| **Void** | "That which has no beginning and no end"; the state past technique | **Body and Mind** (level 20) | Not a Warrior: the class capstone. Dexterity and Wisdom past the mortal cap, the list of limits finished. |

Two things this buys. The four Warriors are four of Musashi's five, and the
fifth is what every Monk becomes at 20 regardless of Warrior, which is the
correct shape for a capstone: the Void is not a school. And it hands the
Elements Warrior its element glyphs an ordering the loader already carries.

✅ Flag positively; nothing needs to change for it to be true. A fifth Warrior,
if ever added, should be asked which book it is, or whether it is the Void made
into a school, which Musashi would call a mistake.

## 📚 4. Wells the dialog did not draw from

None of these is a costume. Each carries a *doctrine* the current text can use.

**Takuan Sōhō, *The Unfettered Mind*** (a Zen abbot's letters to a sword
master, 1630s). The mind that *stops* on anything is caught by it: on the
opponent's blade, on your own fear, on the thought of winning. Freedom is the
mind that does not stop. That is Self-Restoration as doctrine (the mind that
does not stop on fear, charm or poison) and it is the calmest statement of
"the limits are a list, and the list gets shorter" in any literature. Where the
dialog kept the ascetic's stillness as "the held breath", Takuan gives it a
rule: stillness is *not stopping*.

**Bodhidharma at Shaolin.** The legend says he found the monks too weak to sit
in meditation and taught them to move. The body was the *prerequisite* for the
mind, not its enemy. Useful for the Aasimar Monk (§5) and for keeping the
rejected ascetic honestly buried: the contemplative order needed the fighting
art first.

**Hanuman** (the Ramayana; `sangha` reaches `india` at 2). The monkey who
*forgot* he could leap the ocean until Jambavan reminded him. The training arc
run backwards: the power is remembered, not built. A Monk whose school taught
them what they already were. This is the sangha's own martial myth and the
Aasimar Monk's, and it rhymes with Sun Wukong (0018's Candidate C) across the
two wells the sangha reaches (india, china).

**The jianghu.** Wuxia's "rivers and lakes": the parallel society of wanderers,
sects, debts and codes that exists beside the state and answers to none of it.
✅ **The project already generates this**: it is the Secret Orders engine
(`AtlasOfOrders`), with its signs cut into lintels, its salt debts and its
locked rooms. A Monk's school is a jianghu sect, and the Order arc ("nobody is
admitted who cannot demonstrate the work"; "nothing is written; it is walked,
and shown, and walked again") is a training arc told from the outside. The
Monk is the Guild most naturally *from* an Order, and the Order engine could
mint the dojo. Implication, not a fault.

**Destreza's philosophy, not only its circle.** Carranza (1569) and Pacheco de
Narváez built the Spanish school on Euclid and on Neoplatonism: the fencer as
geometer, the fight as a proof. `iberia` is a Dwarf key. ✅ So the
**Dwarf Open Hand Monk is the Destreza Monk**, and the "old fencing masters
drawing circles on the floor" in the proposed Open Hand text are, in this
setting, Dwarves. Never name them; the gear register can hand the Dwarf Monk
a Toledo-steel *jo*.

**The baratero** (*Manual del Baratero*, 1849). The Andalusian knife fighter:
navaja, cloak wrapped on the off arm, the fight in a tavern doorway. `andalus`
is the other Dwarf key. A Dwarf Shadow Monk with a folding knife is the
setting's baratero, and it is the one Iberian martial well besides Destreza
that the ruling (Iberian and Eastern, never Germanic) opens.

**Beni Hasan.** The tombs at Beni Hasan carry the oldest depicted martial art
on record, four thousand years of wrestlers in pairs on a wall, and `egypt` is
a Human key. The Human Monk does not need Japan: the Egyptian well holds
wrestling and *tahtib* stick-fighting, both older than any school the sangha
remembers. Worth knowing so that the Human Monk is not the default ninja.

**Sun Tzu**, in two lines. "Be extremely subtle, even to the point of
formlessness" is the Warrior of Shadow. "To subdue the enemy without fighting is
the acme of skill" is Quivering Palm's restraint: the deadliest technique in the
class carries its own off-switch, and the dialog rightly made that the Open
Hand's soul.

## 📔 5. The Monk inside the setting's metaphysics

The Canon gives each people one organising idea. The Monk has none and leans on
several.

**Nothing given, everything proven.** The Dwarf's metal is *given and must be
proven*. The Celestial's Ideal is *given and cannot bend*. The Monk has nothing
given at all; the whole class is proof without a gift. That makes the Monk the
class that stands furthest from every species' principle, and therefore the one
that reads the same across all ten peoples, which is exactly what the canon
says: "Monks read as ninja across every people." The universality is not a
flattening. It is the class's metaphysics: the body is the one inheritance
nobody's principle touches.

**The Ascending, from the other side.** The Dragon canon describes the sage
"who needs no rule because nothing in them pulls against the way", and then
says the Way of the Ascendant Dragon Monks use the word *Ascending* "and they
mean it". ⚠️ **That Warrior is not on the roster** (the four are Mercy, Open
Hand, Shadow, Elements; the Ascendant Dragon is 2021, not 2024). The canon
references an order that cannot be generated. ✅ **But the Orders engine already
has its slot**: the Primal tradition's devotions include *"a beast that is
always described the same way by people who have never met"*. That is a dragon
that nobody has seen. An Order of the Storm or the Road, Primal, devoted to
that beast, with a Monk in it, *is* the Ascendant Dragon school without a
subclass, and it keeps the canon's rule that nobody in-world explains the
mechanism. Recommend a note in the Dragon canon pointing there, so the next
reader does not mint a subclass to fill a hole the Orders already fill.

**Two no-minds, stated.** The Barbarian page (§4) named the Monk's *mushin* and
the Barbarian's "without thought" as the same absence of hesitation reached by
opposite roads. The proposed Monk text says "faster than doubt, faster than
fear"; the chant says "you lose hesitation". ✅ The difference is now clean and
should be protected: the Barbarian arrives at no-mind by *fullness* (the whole
of the moment), the Monk by *subtraction* (the list gets shorter). Fire and
wind, as the dialog said of Fighter and Monk. Three martial Guilds, three
places the fight is.

**The shadow budget.** The Elves' canon says Fae, Fata and Shadow are one
substance and that the slip ("we shadows") must be used *sparingly, never more
than once in a single piece of text*. The generator can produce a **Shadow Elf,
Shadow background, Warrior of Shadow** in one Character, and the sheet will say
"shadow" as a species, a past and a school. ⚠️ Flag as a contradiction the dice
can create: the canon's rule about frequency is broken by composition, not by
any author. Decree 0005 (affinity steers selection) is the right lever: a mild
negative affinity between the three would keep the word rare without closing a
door. Two of the three together is a story; three is a lecture.

**The Orc entry is already a Monk's.** "Run, be free, and run." Adrenaline Rush
is Step of the Wind with a species name. The wind path, walked at speed. An Orc
Monk needs no reconciliation; the species text was reaching for the class.

## 📔 7. Relationships: backgrounds × Monk

- **Herald.** "You walk in alone, past the guards… If the talking fails, you are
  already inside their lines, and you are the first blade they meet." ✅ That is
  "You arrive with empty hands, and you have never once been unarmed" told from
  the employer's side. The Herald Monk is the class text as a job.
- **Exorcist.** "What works is always liturgical: salt, iron, running water, the
  right words in the right order, which you do not even have to mean." ✅ Named
  techniques that work whether or not you believe. The Exorcist Monk already
  knows the shonen secret: say the name, the thing happens. And the Exorcist's
  enemy makes people "part of something bigger"; the untakeable body is their
  ideal.
- **Spirit Medium × Mercy.** Hand of Ultimate Mercy raises the dead within a day.
  A Medium hears the dead and cannot make them leave. ✅ The one Monk who can
  bring one back, and the queue that forms when the others see it happen. The
  hook's "By morning there is a queue" becomes literal and terrible.
- **Servant × Open Hand.** Quivering Palm: "the touch that waits, days if need
  be." The Servant is still owned: "you had better, darling." ✅ The cupbearer
  who has already touched the master's wrist while pouring, and has not yet
  decided. Chilling, and entirely in the rules.
- **Debunker × Elements.** "The medium's table lifts because of a very strong
  left knee." A Monk who knows exactly how every trick is done, and then
  Empowered Strikes and Elemental Attunement happen to *them*. ✅ "There was no
  mask, and your hand kept going": the Debunker's hook is the Elements Warrior's
  first day.
- **Investigator × Open Hand.** "Observe. Record. Eliminate." That is Destreza's
  method; the circle on the floor is a deduction. ✅ The geometer.
- **Inquisitor × Mercy.** "An instrument does not need to be clean, only sharp."
  ✅ The gods' left hand with a healer's map and a mask. Both gifts in one hand
  is the Inquisitor's whole confession.
- **Gambler.** "You stood up smiling both times. Standing up smiling is the job."
  ✅ Uncanny Metabolism is standing up on Initiative. Deflect Attacks is catching
  the card the dealer palmed.
- **Fated.** "If you fall, you roll over and jump back up." ✅ Slow Fall, verbatim.
- **Destined.** "You can walk further than anyone else in the party." Unarmored
  Movement. Reinforcement, and a funny one: the glowing Monk who cannot be
  followed.
- **Ice Nomad × Elements.** "The ice let you go, and every land since has felt
  thin and overwarm." ✅ Frost as the element that comes back.
- **Naturalist × Elements.** "The natural world does not stop at the edge of the
  page" against "the world has started answering." ✅ Same discovery, two trades.
- **Squire.** "Greatness has logistics." ⚠️ Tension with teeth: the Squire who
  served a Monk carried nothing, because the Monk needed nothing (Self-
  Restoration). What was the Squire *for*? That question is the character.
- **Shadow × Shadow.** ⚠️ Redundant, and worse with a Shadow Elf (§5).
- **Stranger × Shadow.** "Spoken to badly and not answer" plus "stories travel
  ahead of you." ✅ Two ways of being preceded by what people say.
- **Sellsword.** The Monk as labour. Fine. The class needs nothing, which makes a
  Sellsword Monk's fee the only thing they ever ask for, and that is a person.

## 📔 8. Flags

### ✅ Singular, and to be protected

1. **"Speed is freedom: from weight, from distance, from the ground."** The
   thesis, and the exemption doctrine. Every feature line must obey it.
2. **The Fighter echo.** "There is no secret. That is the secret." answered by
   "That is the secret your art keeps." Two schools disagreeing across the page.
   0018 asked for it to be confirmed as a signature. Confirm.
3. **The Elements closing.** "Other students trained until nothing could touch
   them. You trained until everything could be touched." The best single
   sentence in the Monk corpus; the whole Warrior in an inversion.
4. **The Open Hand's courtesy.** "An open hand is a courtesy. It lets everyone
   watch you not making a fist." Destreza's ethic in one line.
5. **The Aasimar Mercy halo** (§6). Already true; never explain it.
6. **The Monk does not reclaim its name** (§2). Keep the indifference.

### ⚠️ Stock, contradictory, or thin

1. **Nothing is wired.** Five texts, none landed; thirty-seven lessons, zero
   inspiration lines; one 2014 blurb on the sheet in the wrong person and voice.
2. **"Psionic energy"** ×2 and the **em-dash** in Implements of Mercy, both
   still live.
3. **Legacy Warrior blurbs** in `Map_of_Classes/Training/Monk.py` render with
   `source=None` and no heading, under the class rules, in third person. Retire
   when 0018's texts land through `extends=`.
4. **The Fighter overlap in the proposed class text.** "One kick, ten thousand
   mornings" and "You did the boring thing every day for years" are one
   sentence in two Guilds. The dialog kept the distinction (endure vs exempt);
   the *class text* should carry it in its own words. Recommend the second
   paragraph's "Then you trained, and one of those stopped being true" do the
   work and the "ten thousand mornings" beat be trimmed to the held breath
   alone.
5. **The shadow budget** (§5): species, background and Warrior can all say
   "shadow" on one sheet.
6. **The Ascendant Dragon** is named in canon and absent from the roster; the
   Orders' Primal devotion already holds the slot (§5).
7. **`ninja` is filed as a society.** It is a profession with a fiction register
   (`anime`) beside it, and the canon separates real cultures from fiction
   registers by law. The influence network makes this harmless (`anime` reaches
   `ninja` at 2, `ninja` reaches `japan` at 2), but the key sits in the wrong
   column and a Dwarf Monk will be handed a Kusarigama before a Toledo *jo*.
   Consider the Monk's society key being *none* and its legend keys being
   `ninja` and `anime` both, so that the species' own culture supplies the
   school and the fiction supplies the idiom.
8. **The Human Monk** is the default ninja unless the Egyptian well is let in.

## 📖 10. Threads to pull in later cycles

- **Fighter**: "Nobody gave you this" and "ten thousand mornings" are one idea
  in two Guilds. When the Fighter page is written, decide which Guild owns the
  grind and which owns the exemption, in the class texts themselves.

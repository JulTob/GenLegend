## 0. The Core Fantasy

> _You only have one moment. One opportunity. To seize everything you ever wanted. One moment. Would you capture it? Or let it slip?_
>
> Idk. Yeesus Probably.

## 📚 1. What a Rogue player is handed today

Four sheets (Human Thief, Elf Assassin, Gnome Arcane Trickster, Tiefling
Soulknife). Each now receives the same core invitation and a different answer
to it: take, wait, stage, or read the opportunity. *Expertise* still says
"Choose two" twice, the Arcane Trickster's legacy entry is still the number 3,
and three of the four draw official one-sentence backgrounds (Charlatan,
Entertainer, Soldier). The fourth draws the Renegade, which is the class's own
background and the only other prose the Gnome receives that sounds like a
person.

## 📜 2. The core fantasy: Opportunity

The burglar and the killer in the crowd are true expressions of the Rogue,
but each belongs to one archetype. The exact moment describes the class's
timing. **Opportunity** describes its meaning: the instant when the world can
still become something else, and the person willing to act before it closes.

A Rogue notices the opportunity, waits for it, takes it, makes it when none is
offered, and passes the opening on. That last movement is essential. The class
does not need a solitary story. Another character's plan, distraction, danger,
or act of courage can be the opening, and the Rogue's answer can create the
next opening for the crew.

This makes adventure itself part of the fantasy. The forbidden door, the
dangerous offer, the stranger with half a map, and the ally already in trouble
are all chances to change what happens next. A character guided by Opportunity
has a reason to accept the call and bring everyone through it.

The rules carry that rhythm:

| Feature (level) | What it proves |
|---|---|
| Sneak Attack (1) | Extra damage with a Finesse or Ranged weapon when the attack has Advantage, or when a non-Incapacitated ally stands within 5 feet of the target; Disadvantage closes both routes. The opening can be yours or one a companion creates. |
| Cunning Action (2) | Dash, Disengage, or Hide as a Bonus Action. You reach an opening before it closes and leave danger without abandoning the scene. |
| Steady Aim (3) | Give up movement and let the opening come to you. |
| Cunning Strike (5) | Trade damage dice for effects. You turn one opening into the next. |
| Uncanny Dodge, Evasion (5, 7) | You deny danger the opportunity it thought it had. |
| **Reliable Talent** (7) | *Nine or lower is a ten* on trained checks. Readiness lets you act when the moment arrives. |
| Slippery Mind, Elusive (15, 18) | You protect your own choices and deny enemies their clean shot. |
| **Stroke of Luck** (20) | A failed D20 Test becomes a 20. The closed opportunity opens once more. |

The Rogue is **the class of *kairos***: the opportune moment, the gap, the
occasion. The Greeks made him a god with a forelock and a bald skull, because
you grasp the moment as it comes toward you and find nothing to hold once it
has passed (Lysippos carved him; Posidippus wrote the epigram). Athens is a
Celestial key, so the Rogue's patron figure is one the Celestials would
recognise and could never be. ✅ Expertise, Cant, tools, and practiced hands
are the readiness that lets a mortal recognise Kairos and choose.

**The hard edge.** Reliable Talent is the opportunity you prepared for. Stroke
of Luck is the one that should have passed and somehow did not. The pícaro can
never quite tell the crew which was skill, which was nerve, and which was the
world looking away at the right time.

## 📔 3. The register: the shared picaresque

The project's rule is one register per class, and the set so far runs chant,
training memoir, shonen arc, warm scripture, the oath remembered, the terms,
sensation, field diary, told tale, adventure novel, the Dao De Jing, the
maker's log. The shared picaresque fills the Rogue's place in that set and
answers the standing preference for Iberian and Eastern wells.

✅ **The picaresque is the one novel form Iberia invented**, and it is the
Rogue's: *Lazarillo de Tormes* (1554), *Guzmán de Alfarache*, Quevedo's
*Buscón*. First-person, episodic, the *pícaro* surviving by wit among masters
who deserve him, addressed to a "Your Grace" who has asked for an
explanation. Its tone is the one the class needs: wry, unsentimental, funny
about hunger, dignified about small victories, and never once sorry.

Opportunity changes the address. In the house's second person, the confession
becomes **a shared conspiracy**. The Rogue is telling the crew how the trick
worked and quietly acknowledging the glance, raised voice, held door, or brave
mistake that made it possible. The picaresque supplies the voice. Opportunity
supplies the direction and keeps that voice turned toward the adventure.

Distinct from the neighbours: the Bard's told tale performs; the pícaro
*reports*. The Warlock confesses to a crack; the Rogue lets companions in on
the method. The Fighter's "nobody gave you this" is earned; the Rogue sees
what nobody meant to give and turns it into a way forward. The Renegade
background already begins there: "Nobody gave you anything. You took a corner
of the world."

## 📚 4. Wells, on the culture keys the setting already holds

**The Treasure of Rhampsinitus** (`egypt`, Humans; Herodotus II.121). The
oldest heist on record: two brothers rob the pharaoh's treasury by a secret
stone; one is trapped, the other cuts off his head so the body cannot be
identified, then steals the body back from under the guards by getting them
drunk, and is finally given the princess for being the cleverest man alive.
✅✅ Every Thief feature is in it (Second-Story Work, Fast Hands, Supreme
Sneak, Thief's Reflexes), and it is on the Human key nobody uses.

**Lazarillo** (`iberia`, Dwarves). The blind master, the priest with the
locked bread-chest, the squire with nothing. ✅ The Dwarf Rogue is the pícaro
proper, and the Dwarf entry *absolves the thief in advance*: "Come back home
with enough gold, and your clan will hail you as a hero, no matter your past
transgressions." The Dwarf Thief who robs a bank-cathedral is the species'
own kind of hero.

**Odysseus** (`homeric`, Goliaths). *Polytropos*, of many turns; grandson of
Autolycus the master thief, who was Hermes' son. The horse is an Assassin's
ambush and a Thief's infiltration in one. ✅ The Goliath Rogue is Odysseus,
which inverts the size joke, and against the Goliath temper ("solves the wrong
problem, thoroughly") the Goliath Rogue is the one Goliath who reasons first.

**Loki and Gwydion** (`norse`, `celt`, Elves). The thief of Sif's hair and
Idunn's apples; the Mabinogion's magician who conjures horses from mushrooms
to steal pigs. ✅ Two Arcane Tricksters on the Elves' keys, and the Dark Elf's
"ambushers who came from below" is the species entry's Assassin.

**The Water Margin** (`china`, Fae). The hundred and eight outlaws of
Liangshan: the brotherhood that is more honest than the court it fled. ✅ The
Renegade background's crew ("Nobody sells out their own. Ever.") has an
Eastern epic, and the Fae Elf Rogue belongs to it.

**Ishikawa Goemon** (`japan`, Dragonborn). The noble thief boiled alive in a
cauldron for trying to kill the regent, and still played on every stage. ✅
The Dragonborn Rogue is Goemon, *not* the shinobi: the canon gives the ninja
to the Monk, and the two classes should not share a costume.

**Reynard** (`germany`, Gnomes). The fox of the beast epic, who wins every
trial by talking. ✅ The Gnome Arcane Trickster (seed 65, with the Renegade)
is Reynard: "through a lock before they finish arguing about the noise".

**Huehuecóyotl** (`aztec`, Dragonborn). The old coyote, trickster and
musician. The Dragonborn's second well gives its Rogue a second face.

**Manjushri's sword** (`sangha`, Aasimar). The bodhisattva of wisdom carries a
flaming sword that cuts through ignorance. ✅✅ The Soulknife's Psychic Blade
is a sword made of mind that cuts minds (Rend Mind stuns), and the least
"rogue" of the Rogues has an Eastern anchor that is exact: the blade that
cuts illusion, held by the pensive kind's child who took the running trade.

**Hermes.** The Cleric page gave the Trickery Domain Athena; Hermes stays
with the Rogue: thief on his first day, god of roads, heralds and thresholds.
The Orders' Road domain is his, and the Veil domain is where the Rogue's
guild lives (§5).

Together the wells give Opportunity a repertoire rather than a costume.
Rhampsinitus takes it. Odysseus plans it. The outlaws of the Water Margin
share it. Goemon risks it. Reynard stages it. Manjushri gives thought an edge.
Hermes crosses the threshold it opens. Kairos remains the instant itself.

## 📔 5. The Rogue inside the setting's metaphysics

**The Veil domain is the Rogue's Order.** *"There is a mark chalked low on
walls in every city, and it tells you which door is safe tonight."* Its four
facets are four Rogue lives: the Kind Lie, the Watcher ("what is done in the
dark is done honestly, because no one is performing"), the Second Face, the
Night Itself. The Wall domain's Locksmith ("you cannot build a door worth
trusting until you have opened every kind there is") is the Thief's. ✅
Thieves' Cant and the Orders' chalk marks are one system seen from two
Atlases. Each mark is an opportunity one stranger left for another. Each coded
sentence tells the crew where the opening is without telling the room.

**The shadow budget, fourth site.** The Monk page counted Shadow Elf, Shadow
background and Warrior of Shadow. The Rogue adds the `shadow` gear theme
(Misericorde, Sleeve Knife, Quiet Answer, Alley Snap). ⚠️ A Shadow Elf Rogue
with the Shadow background is handed a Quiet Answer. Three "shadow"s on one
sheet by composition; the Decree 0005 nudge applies here too.

**The Aasimar Rogue is a walking confession** (Aasimar page §6). Justice's halo
dims at every lie; Truth's scatters at every strong feeling. The Rogue is the
Aasimar's hardest class and therefore its best, and the generated Assassin of
Aidos (Shame) is the roster's own joke about it.

**The Celestial Reaper** is the Assassin's named form, and the kit already
carries its tell. Sacrifice's aureola *"darkens for a moment whenever you see
someone die"*, and the Assassin's trade is the first blow that finishes: a halo
that darkens at every kill is a tally the Reaper cannot stop keeping. Justice's
Reaper is the other face, since that halo *"dims when you are not being
honest"*, so the Assassin who kills without a disguise keeps a perfect ring and
the one who wears a face pays for it above the face.

**The Tiefling and Elusive.** *"No attack roll can have Advantage against
you."* The Tiefling entry: "once in a while somebody takes a swing in a
crowded square and not one hand moves to stop it." ✅ The one who has been
watched all their life is the one nobody gets the drop on. And the Soulknife's
Psychic Whispers (seed 67) give a people with no "we" their first crew that
speaks without speaking.

**Reliable Talent and the Dwarf's metal.** "Metal shapes in the forge. People
in the challenge." Reliable Talent is a person who has been shaped until the
work cannot fail. The Dwarf Rogue's professionalism is the species' metaphysic
in a trade the species would not name in front of the Saints.

**The ninja belongs to the Monk.** Canon: "Monks read as ninja across every
people." The Rogue must not be the second shinobi; the Assassin's model is
Goemon, the Water Margin, Rhampsinitus, the Old Man of the Mountain if a
Levantine key is ever playable. Keep the kunai out of the Rogue's gear theme.

## 📔 7. Relationships: backgrounds × Rogue

- **Gambler.** *"Gambling has nothing to do with luck… You were counting."*
  ✅✅ Reliable Talent as a background: nine or lower is a ten because you were
  counting. And Stroke of Luck is the one hand that *was* luck, which the
  Gambler will never admit.
- **Renegade.** The class's own. *"You fight like someone who cannot afford to
  lose."* Sneak Attack, described by someone who has never read the rule.
  ⚠️ Redundant with the class the way Wildkeeper is with the Druid; the
  interesting Renegade is a Paladin (Paladin page §7).
- **Servant.** *"You knew where the silver was hidden, and the guests left
  generous tips."* ✅ A Thief's sentence sitting in a cupbearer's story. The
  Servant Rogue took the silver on the way out.
- **Bailiff.** *"How a document should look, which is also how to make one
  look right when the file is thin."* Forgery Kit. ✅ The Bailiff is a Rogue by
  trade; the Bailiff Rogue serves warrants by night, and the Badge is the
  best disguise on the roster.
- **Investigator × Arcane Trickster.** *"Observe. Record. Eliminate."* The
  detective is the Rogue's mirror, and the great detective was a boxer and a
  lockpick. ✅ A reasoner with an invisible hand.
- **Inquisitor.** Grants Thieves' Tools. Already half a Rogue: the left hand
  picks locks.
- **Archaeologist.** *"You have gone through a window at speed with a lamp in
  your teeth."* ✅ Second-Story Work, verbatim.
- **Tomb Raider.** *"Which floor is a lie."* Fast Hands on a trap. The Thief
  underground.
- **Exorcist × Thief.** *"The right words in the right order, which you do not
  even have to mean."* ✅✅ That is Use Magic Device: the Thief reads the scroll
  without understanding it, and it works. The Exorcist Thief has been doing
  this for years with salt and iron.
- **Fated.** Stroke of Luck: the cursed one's single 20. ✅ "If you fall, you
  roll over and jump back up" is Uncanny Dodge.
- **Stranger.** *"Which crossings ask for papers and which guards can be
  talked past."* ✅ Thieves' Cant as the diaspora's cant; the Long Memory as a
  fence.
- **Herald × Assassin.** *Infiltration Expertise*: mimic a hand or a voice
  after an hour. The Herald who forges the treaty, or delivers it "past the
  guards, into the room where the thing is decided" and is "the first blade
  they meet". ✅ The background wrote the Assassin's job as diplomacy.
- **Survivor × Assassin.** *"This time you are ready."* Assassinate's first
  round, reversed: the one who was ambushed becomes the ambush.
- **Spirit Medium × Soulknife.** *"There has been no silence in a long, long
  time."* Psychic Whispers from the other side; the medium with a blade of
  thought.
- **Hermeticist × Arcane Trickster.** Spell Thief at 17: "take one more secret
  out of a hand that has held it too long" is the Forge domain's Fire Thief
  facet, and the Hermeticist's "the one thing you still cannot produce" is
  finally stolen rather than made.
- **Fortune Teller.** *"Fingers quick enough to keep the trick invisible."*
  The Rogue's hands in another trade.
- **Debunker × Arcane Trickster.** The trick that is a trick and also magic.
- **Vagabond.** Sleight of Hand; "quicker to make an exit". The pícaro's
  road.
- **Guardian.** The Rogue as bodyguard: the Veil's Sheathed facet, "the best
  guard is the one whose charge never learns how close it came".
- **Agent of the Ninth Quill.** The Arcane Trickster's own background, and
  one sentence long. ⚠️ Needs the treatment the other custom backgrounds got.
- **Official backgrounds.** Charlatan (*False Identity*), Criminal (*Criminal
  Contact*), Wayfarer (*The Overlooked*), Soldier (*Old Company*): the
  officials most natural to a Rogue (Backgrounds-Official §3).

## 📖 9. Text

*House rules: second person; the picaresque's tone (wry, unsentimental, never
sorry); no proper nouns; no em-dashes; no open choices. The class text moves
from noticing to taking, making, and sharing an opportunity. Each archetype
owns one relationship to that theme.*

| Archetype | Relationship to Opportunity | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Thief** | Takes it before it closes. | Active. |
| **Assassin** | Waits until the true opening stands alone. | Active. |
| **Arcane Trickster** | Stages it by controlling what the room watches. | Active; current. |
| **Soulknife** | Reads it while it moves, then passes each new answer through the crew. | Active; current. |
| **Swashbuckler** | Makes it by provoking attention and setting the tempo. | Future; not generated. |

The framework can grow without blurring. A future archetype might plan, risk,
expose, bargain with, or refuse an opportunity, but its rules must earn the
verb.

### Class text (current)

```
Luck may offer an opening. Opportunity is what you do with it. The moment demands an answer. You know the answer. You do not let it close. You do not waste your shot.

You know the game. You know the board. You move first. Sometimes you wait. Sometimes you taunt. Sometimes you win before the room knows. Sometimes you are gone before you lose. And when no opening comes, you make one: a word, a flourish, a risk taken so confidently that everyone mistakes it for a plan. Call it half skill, half luck. All of it is why you play.
```

### Thief: takes the opportunity (current)

```
You take the opportunity. A lock leaves a question in the door. Your hands answer. By the time the guards come, you are already on the roof. While everyone else decides whether the thing can be done, you jump in.

Guarded things, strange things, magical things: you take them. You do not need a proper entrance. You need a loose hinge, a turned head, one locked door. Crew's ready. Lock's open. Prize's moving. You are already gone.
```

### Assassin: waits for the opportunity (current)

```
You understand the courage of waiting. Patience is not inaction. It is the waiting game: attention held steady while false openings come and go. You learn the room, the route, the hand, the voice. Stay still. Stay ready. You move first. Game over.
```

### Arcane Trickster: stages the opportunity (current)

```
You stage the opportunity. Every eye is part of the trick. First, the setup: an empty hand, an easy smile, nothing to hide. Then, the distraction: a flash, a flourish, the one hand everyone watches. Then, the turn: a coin changes hands, a card switches places, or a rose appears. But the trick is somewhere nobody is looking. The purse changes pockets, a key slips away, or the wrong envelope is given. Half sleight of hand. Half spell. Which half was which? They never know.

The room watches the show. Your crew watches the opening. The lookout, the muscle, and the distraction. They all know their signs. One nod. One step. By the time anyone looks where they should, the lock is open, the prize is gone, and the crew is through. You show the empty hand. You take the bow. That is the prestige.
```

### Soulknife: reads the opportunity (current)

```
You read the opportunity. Three ways in. Two ways out. One chance. One look, next move. One change, new game. Your plan travels at the speed of thought. No words. No pause. Your crew moves as quickly as you think.

They search for steel. Let them. Your mind is the instrument. Empty hands tell a lovely lie. A thought takes an edge. The room changes; you change faster. What looks like the mistake is the gambit. What looks like the retreat puts your crew behind them. By the time they understand, they are standing in the trap and falling.
```

# QST-0046.8 — Implements: weapons that attack with INT, WIS or CHA

- **Status:** Open — implemented 2026-08-05, **partially lost**
- **Owner:** unclaimed
- **Route to:** Workshop (Artificer)
- **Parent:** QST-0046 · **Related:** QST-0046.7 · QST-0076

## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

Julio: *"for magic users it would make sense to have some weapons that attack
with int/wis/cha, to make them more usable… It should be clearly described in the
description which one is using."*

A Wizard holding a Quarterstaff at Strength 8 is a joke the sheet plays on its
owner. Five implements in `Ledger_of_Wonders` let a caster fight with the score
their whole build is about: Runeblade (INT), Oath Mace (WIS), Beguiler's Rapier
(CHA), Warden's Staff (WIS), Hexbolt Wand (CHA).

Design constraints:

- They are **Simple** weapons on purpose. An implement makes a caster usable in
  melee; it does not hand them martial training they never had.
- `attack_with` is a **field**, printed by `blurb()` on its own line, never left
  to prose. Julio asked for it to be stated, and a weapon that quietly used a
  different score would be a trap.
- `_fit_implement` buys one at level 5+, out of leftovers only, and puts it **in
  hand** — the mundane melee weapon steps aside to the belt. Leaving it slung was
  the same joke with an extra step.
- This forced splitting proficiency from the catalogue: `trained_for()` answers
  by **Tag**, because an implement is a Simple weapon that lives outside
  `Ledger_of_Weapons` and `weapon_pool()` is necessarily a catalogue.

**Two pieces did not survive.** `Item.__init__` lost its `attack_with = ""`
default, and `blurb()` lost the line that prints it. The field is still set by
`Make_Weapon`, so a caster's implement **silently uses a different ability with
nothing on the sheet saying so** — precisely the trap Julio asked to avoid.

## 🧾 Evidence

- `python -m AtlasInventarium.Ledger_of_Wonders` → `AssertionError` on
  `f"Attacks with {implement.attack_with}" in implement.blurb()`; the blurb
  returns only `"1d8 Force, Versatile (1d10). A {material} blade …"`.
- `grep attack_with Grimoire_of_Items.py` → present in `Make_Weapon` only;
  absent from `Item.__init__` and from the `blurb` body.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

`Item.__init__` defaults `attack_with = ""`; `blurb()` prints
`"Attacks with <Ability>"` between the damage line and the mastery line. The
existing assertion then guards it.

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer

Small, safe and self-guarding — the self-test already encodes the contract.
Worth doing ahead of the bigger restores because the current state is *wrong on
the sheet*, not merely missing.

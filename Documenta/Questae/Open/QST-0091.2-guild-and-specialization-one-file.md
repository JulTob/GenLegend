## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

Adding a Guild today touches, at least: `Make_Guild` (bytecode until the 65edc0f restore lands), the `AtlasOfGuilds/__init__` library list, `Scroll_of_Constants.classes()`, `Codex_of_Progression.get_class_progression`'s mapping, `Grimoire_of_Health` (a table **and** a twelve-branch string ladder), `TrainingKit`'s module list, `Grimoire_of_Spellcasters` (a `Spellcaster` subclass with slot and prepared tables, an `elif _bears(character, "…")` ladder and an HTML block), a legacy `Training/<Guild>.py` if-ladder, and an `AtlasOfTraining/Map_of_<Guild>_Training.py`. Twenty-eight files name Paladin outside its kit.

Adding a Specialization touches its kit (right, 54 `Make_Specialization` calls), plus `Scroll_of_Constants.subclasses()` (stale), `SUBCLASS_CASTING_ABILITY`, the legacy ladder keyed on `Subclass == "Evoker"`, and a Training map gated on a `path=` string. Both engines run for every Character: TrainingKit first, the legacy ladder fills gaps.

## 🧾 Evidence

- `grep -rlw Paladin Atlas* app` minus its own kit and training files → 28.
- `Grimoire_of_Health`: `HIT_DIE_TABLE`, `hit_dice()` ladder, and `guild_hit_die` in GuildKit: three sources of one number.
- `Codex_of_Progression` lines 26 to 40: the mapping is typed by hand.
- Eleven of thirteen kits are 33-line name lists; FighterKit (1,031 lines) is the only one that declares FEATURES, CHOICES and RESOURCES on the Tag.
- `Grimoire_of_Characters.apply_class_features`: `Apply_Guild_Trainings` then `filter_legacy_features(class_prog.features(...))`.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

One kit per Guild declares the class as a Tag and its Specializations as classes in the same file: hit die, saves, armour and weapon training, vocation, leanings, casting (ability, progression, spell list), per-level FEATURES, CHOICES and RESOURCES as Reports, prose beside them. A Specialization is a class statement whose `FEATURES(tag, inherited)` appends to its Guild's. The Guild catalogue is the class tree under `Guild`; the Specialization catalogue is the tree under each Guild. `classes()`, `subclasses()`, `HIT_DIE_TABLE`, the `hit_dice` ladder, the `Codex_of_Progression` mapping, `SUBCLASS_CASTING_ABILITY` and the `_bears` ladder are deleted. Spell slots and prepared counts are Reports on the casting Guild, read by one Spellcasting projection instead of thirteen `Spellcaster` subclasses. Each legacy `Training/<Guild>.py` retires when `training_covers()` reports full coverage.

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer

- Blocked on the Guild body returning as source (Integration Plan step 1) and on the TagKit 0.2 pin (step 7) for inherited Reports.
- Order inside the axis: Wizard first (five Specializations, simplest casting), Cleric (voice already bound), then the rest; Fighter and Barbarian are done.
- Keep `Make_Guild` only if a class statement cannot express the same declaration as readably.

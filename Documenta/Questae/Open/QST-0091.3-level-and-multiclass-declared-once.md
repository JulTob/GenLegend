## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

What a level grants is spread across four kinds of place:

1. **Each Guild's per-level features** are an if-ladder in `Map_of_Classes/Training/<Guild>.py` (`if level >= 3: … if subclass == "Evoker": …`), with the same universal steps repeated in every ladder: feats at 4, 8, 12, 16, the Epic Boon at 19 (80 `ApplyRandomFeats` / `ApplyEpicBoon` call sites across thirteen files).
2. **Hit dice** in three places; **proficiency bonus** in `Map_of_Scores.Proficiency`; **spell slots and prepared counts** per caster class inside `Grimoire_of_Spellcasters`.
3. **The TOP side** has the right substrate: `Join_Guild(levels=)`, a per-Guild level ledger, `guilds_on`, `Multiclassed`, and `Make_Guild(multiclass_gains=…)`.
4. **Multiclass** is machinery without a producer: the Player generator calls `Apply_Guild` once; `Training/Multiclass.py` is a two-class stub; no Guild declares its multiclass prerequisites (2024: ability minimums per class) or its caster progression (full, half, third, pact), so the shared spell-slot table for multiclass casters has no inputs.

## 🧾 Evidence

- `grep -c 'ApplyRandomFeats\|ApplyEpicBoon' Training/*.py` → 80.
- `Training/Wizard.py` lines 15 to 60: the ladder, with HTML in strings.
- `Map_of_Character_Generation.py`: no `Join_Guild`, no `Multiclass`.
- The Guild source at 65edc0f documents "multiclass dip" and `multiclass_gains` but records no prerequisites and no progression kind.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

The universal level rules live in one place, as data: feat levels, boon level, proficiency bonus by level, the multiclass spell-slot table. Each Guild declares its own per-level grants as a tuple on its Tag (QST-0091.2) plus three multiclass Reports: `PREREQUISITES` (ability minimums), `MULTICLASS_GAINS` (what a dip grants), `CASTER_PROGRESSION` (full, half, third, pact, none). A Character's level table is then computed: the union of its Guild ledgers under the rules (Extra Attack does not stack; caster levels sum by progression into the shared slot table; a Guild's grants apply at that Guild's rank, the universal grants at total level). The generator can build a multiclass Character through `Join_Guild(levels=)` when a request asks for it, and the sheet reads as one life, not two lists. The thirteen ladders are deleted when `training_covers()` reports full coverage.

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer

- Depends on QST-0091.2's Guild shape. Decide the three multiclass Reports and the universal-rules module here; decide whether the Player front offers multiclass at all in this cut (Decree 0004 scope) in a Dialog.
- Decree 0002 keeps `tier` on the Character root; the Guild ledger is where per-Guild levels live. Do not add a second level field.
- Affinity (Decree 0005) already knows the casting ability per Guild; caster progression belongs beside it.

## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

`Make_Background` is already one call: name, audiences, abilities, skills, tools, Origin Feat, title, description, source. Seventy-six Backgrounds are declared through it and no parallel registry exists. What remains outside:

- 12 files name Farmer or Hermeticist outside BackgroundKit and AtlasOfBackgrounds: `Map_of_Stories` (two copies), `Map_of_Titles` (two), `GearKit`, `Grimoire_of_Objects`, `Map_of_Materials`, `Map_of_Exotic_Familiars`, `character_sheet.py`, and the legacy `Map_of_Backgrounds` (read only by the retired Flask routes).
- The audience classification (Player, NonPlayer) is applied as Pins inside `Make_Background`; under TagKit 0.2.0a3 the root `Background_Audience` needs the `@Pin` mark (QST-0093.5), nothing else changes.
- Background-specific production (a Merchant's wares, a Farmer's tools) is not yet routed outward; Current-State names this as the approved destination not yet complete.

**Correction (2026-09-14).** The one call is not the whole declaration. Before the 2026-08-29 wipe, each official background also carried a hook title and a Hook, with `title` as the background's own name. The post-wipe `Make_Background` has no hook parameters, so the sixteen officials lost their Hooks, and fifteen of the post-wipe records put an old hook title in `title`. Their settled text is on `Documenta/Canon/Mythos/Backgrounds-Official.md`; the code defect is Repairs-Ledger B17.

## 🧾 Evidence

- `grep -rlwE 'Hermeticist|Farmer' Atlas* app | grep -vE 'BackgroundKit|AtlasOfBackgrounds'` → 12 files.
- `BackgroundKit.py` line 769: "Construct one complete Background Shape and apply its Pins"; Field reads `Background_Audience[:]`, `Available[:]`, `NPC_Background[:]` at lines 233, 1953, 2260 to 2270.
- Dry run under TagKit 0.2.0a3 reaches BackgroundKit only after GuildKit's `Report(value)` site; its audience Pins need only the mark.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

One declaration per Background stays the shape, and so do the audience Pins and their Fields. Anything a Background contributes to stories, titles, gear or materials is declared on the Background itself (a `WARES` or `TRADE` Report, a Kinship-like `Trade` Flag) and the twelve files ask the Tag. `Map_of_Backgrounds` is deleted with the Flask routes.

- The `@Pin` mark is one line on the root; the 76 declarations do not move.

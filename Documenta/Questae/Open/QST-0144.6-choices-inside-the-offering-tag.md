## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

A Character carries its own random source, the **Dice Bag**: `char.Dice_Bag( purpose )` opens a fresh stream keyed on the Character's seed and a purpose string, so two Characters never share a stream and a choice does not move when an unrelated choice is added before it. That is Decree 0005's design, and the project's law since (ruling 8). Measured on the branch after station 4, the law holds in only about half of the places a build chooses something. The inventory (`QST-0144.6-choice-inventory.md`) counts **104 live choice sites**:

| How the choice is drawn today | Sites | What is wrong with it |
|---|---|---|
| A Dice Bag with a hand-written purpose (`"identity.species.Elf.keen_senses"`, `"training.monk.open_hand.bullet"`, `"wizard.spellbook"`) | 49 | Right mechanism, three naming styles, and the name says where the code was when it was written, not which Tag offers the choice |
| `Pick` or `Accept` with no purpose: the purpose is **derived from the caller's stack frame** (`module.function`, plus a per-Character counter `#0`, `#1`) | 14 | Magic: renaming or moving a function moves the draws. Worse, a bare `Accept` always derives *"CharactersKit.Character.Accept"*, so every bare Accept of a Character shares **one** stream and a counter, whatever Tag called it |
| The `random` module or `app.random`, inside `Isolated_Legacy_RNG`, which seeds both global streams with the Character's seed around the whole summon | 38 | Deterministic per seed, but one shared stream in draw order: any new draw anywhere, even a story sentence, shifts every later legacy pick. It is why the words of station 3 moved a Fighter line's feat picks |
| A private `random.Random( f"{seed}:{salt}" )` (gear, the Cleric's Divine Order and Blessed Strikes) | 2 | A second Dice Bag design, with its own key shape |
| Other (the Student of War shuffle helper, taking a purpose parameter) | 1 | Fine; it takes the lesson's name once the lesson names it |

Where the choice is **made** is the other half of the straddle: 19 Tag-owned choices are made by an outer rite (`Apply_Fighting_Styles`, `Apply_Specialization`, `Apply_Casting_Variant`, `_random_heritage`, the Species resolvers for Keen Senses, Versatile, Giant Heritage and Celestial Descent, `get_spellcaster`, the Monk bullets, Primal Knowledge, the Human culture) instead of inside the Imprint of the Tag that offers it, as Decree 0009 point 4 and the seven-slot contract of Dialog 0027 ask.

Ten purposes belong to no Tag and are right as they are: the five identity draws that *precede* their Tag (alignment, species, gender, guild, background), the ability scores, the story, the title and its fallback, the naming fallback. The naming engine (eleven random-module sites), the gear loadout (one private stream with eight salts) and the common language pick are Character rites, not Tags: they move to Dice Bags with `identity.name.*`, `gear.*` and `identity.languages` purposes, which Decree 0005 already named.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

Every choice of a Character's build is drawn from the Character's Dice Bag, opened with a purpose named by the Tag that offers the choice: `<Tag class name>.<choice>`, for example `Fighting_Style.choice`, `Elf.heritage`, `Keen_Senses.choice`, `Student_of_War.tool`, `Boon_of_Fate.ability`. No purpose is derived from a stack frame; a bare `Pick` or `Accept` is refused with a message that names the rule. The random module and `app.random` are not reached while a Character is built, and `Isolated_Legacy_RNG` is deleted. Every Tag-owned choice is made inside that Tag's Imprint, from a pool that respects what the Character already holds, applying the chosen Tags. The seeds move **once**, declared.

## 🛠️ The plan (one branch, four commits, in this order)

1. **The rule, in one place.** `Character.Pick_Bag` stops reading the caller's frame; `Pick` and `Accept` without `purpose=` or `dice=` raise a `ValueError` naming ruling 8. `Dice_Bag` is unchanged. A new gate, `scripts/verify_dice_purposes.py`, runs the wide grid with a recording hook on `Dice_Bag` and asserts: no purpose carries a frame-derived `#` counter, no two Tags open the same purpose, and the set of purposes is the one this questa declares (so a new choice must be declared to pass). The 14 derived sites fail loudly until commit 2 names them.
2. **Every Tag-owned purpose renamed at once** (32 rename-only rows plus the names inside the 19 move rows), the twelve collisions resolved as the inventory says (`Spellbook.form` and `Wizard.Spellcasting.known`, `Celestial.patron` and `Celestial.patron.name`, `<Boon>.ability` per Boon, one name per Ranger lesson, `<Feat>.casting_ability` threaded to the eight Origin Feats, `Rogue.Expertise.choice` / `Bard.Expertise.choice` / `Ranger.Expertise.choice`, `identity.name.<strategy>.base`, `identity.languages` for the common pick and `<Guild>.language` for the grants). Versions and namespaces collapse to the defaults (`"1"`, `"GenLegend"`), so a purpose is one string.
3. **The 43 draws that bypass the Dice Bag** become Dice Bag draws named by their Tag or, for the engines, by Decree 0005's `identity.name.*` / `gear.*` names; the two private `random.Random` helpers (`_fork_rng`, `gear_stream`) become `char.Dice_Bag` calls; the duplicate Druid Primal Order draw is deleted; `Isolated_Legacy_RNG` and `app/random.py` are deleted when nothing reaches them during a summon (the gate of commit 1 proves it: a recording hook on `random.random` must see no call).
4. **Every Tag-owned choice is made at the Tag or in the Tag's file.** Julio's rule (2026-10-06): *"Tag-owned choices should be at tag level or helper functions inside the tag file."* So a choice lives either on the Tag itself (its Imprint or an Action) or in a helper function in the file that declares the Tag, called from the Tag; never in an outer rite in another file (`Grimoire_of_Characters`, `Grimoire_of_Features`, the legacy builders of `Map_of_Classes/Training`, `get_spellcaster`). A choice offered by a whole family (which Fighting Style, which Invocation, which spells) lives in the family root's file (`FeatKit` beside `Fighting_Style_Feat`, `InvocationKit`, the Spellcasting lesson's file), and each Shape that offers it calls that helper. This pass moves the 19 rows the inventory marks "both move and rename" and every other row whose "where made" is not the Tag's own file: the Fighting Style, the Specialization and the Casting Variant into the Guild's file; the Heritage into the Species'; Keen Senses, Versatile, Giant Heritage and Celestial Descent into their Traits' files; Weapon Mastery, Mystic Arcanum, the Eldritch Knight's spells and the spells known into the Spellcasting lessons' file; the Monk bullets, the Stars' star map and omen, the patrons' names into their Specializations' files; Primal Knowledge into its lesson; the Human culture into `Human`. Because each bag is keyed by its purpose and not by draw order, moving a call site changes nothing a bag yields; this commit therefore reads **identical** on the gates of commit 3, unless a pool now respects what the Character holds at a different moment, which is declared per family.

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer

- **A purpose is a stable string that no file move or function rename can change.** That is Decree 0005's own rule; the frame derivation broke it quietly. Refusing a bare draw is the only way a new choice cannot slip back into magic.
- **Name by the Tag's class name, not its display name.** `Fighting_Style.choice`, never `"Fighting Style.choice"`: the class name is the identifier in code; the display name is for the sheet and the words.
- **The name in a purpose is the name a reader types** to refer to the Tag: the identifier the Map or Kit binds (`Boon_of_Fate.ability`, `Eldritch_Invocations.choice`, `OpenHand.bullet`, `BattleMaster.maneuvers`). Where a factory mints a different `__name__` (`BoonOfFate`), the typed name wins; making the factories mint the typed name is a follow-up.
- **A choice made again at a later level is another event**, and gets another bag: `General_Feat.choice.4`, `General_Feat.choice.8`, `Fighting_Style.choice.10`. A repeat that no level tells apart carries the occurrence the caller passes, `Skilled.choice.2`. No hidden counter anywhere.
- **One bag, one choice.** Where one helper served several Tags (`_pick_mental_casting_ability`, `_ability_to_increase`, the Ranger named options) the Tag is threaded in and the bag is the Tag's. Where one choice was drawn twice from two streams (the Blessed Warrior cantrips, the Druid Primal Order) the second draw is deleted.
- **The move of commit 4 keeps the rite order's dependencies**, listed in the inventory: the Heritage Imprint sets the lineage spellcasting ability that `get_spellcaster` reads later; the Battle Master's `after=` hook applies the Maneuver Tags once the Specialization transaction has committed; skills exist before the Student of War draws a skill.
- **Not in this station:** the NonPlayer path (Decree 0004), the Monk focus glyph (not reached on the Player sheet; renamed when the Monk Kit is ported), the three Origin Feat draws whose `awaken` stops before them (the Origin Feat family port fixes the dead code first).

# QST-0093.13 — Tag factories are made, not built: `Build_` becomes `Make_`

- **Type:** chore · naming
- **Priority:** 🟡 normal
- **Status:** Solved (2026-09-24)
- **Owner:** unclaimed
- **Route to:** Simplicity (Monk)
- **Parent:** QST-0093 · **Related:** Decree 0009 point 9 · QST-0093.14

---

## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

Decree 0009 gives the word **build** to the walk's result (`Find_Build( char )`). Twenty functions that *declare Tags* were also called `Build_…`, so a reader could confuse making a Tag with reading a build.

## 🛠️ What changed (commit `5935402`)

A mechanical rename, `Build_X` → `Make_X`, for every factory: `Make_Guild`, `Make_Specialization`, `Make_Casting_Variant`, `Make_Training`, `Make_Background`, `Make_General_Feat`, `Make_Fighting_Style`, `Make_Epic_Boon`, `Make_Catalogued_Origin_Feat`, `Make_Invocation`, `Make_Item`, `Make_Armour`, `Make_Shield`, `Make_Worn`, `Make_Consumable`, `Make_Weapon`, `Make_Pack`, `Make_Craft`, `Make_Feature_Tag`, and the alias `Make_Myth`.

- Renamed in all 53 Python modules, the living Canon, and the open and working questae (67 files).
- Left as written: Decrees, Dialogs, solved questae, transcripts and the recovery vault. They are records of what was said at the time.

## 🧾 Evidence

Fingerprint 273/273 identical; wide sweep 1164/1164; replay exact; kit self-tests unchanged (16 pass; the same 5 fail at the same lines as on `main`).

## ❓ Questions

None left. The one choice made without asking: historical documents keep the old names. Say so if you want them changed too.

## ✅ Resolution
- **Decided by:** Julio (Decree 0009 point 9; "rebuild to make", 2026-09-24)
- **What changed:** see above
- **Practice/preference to remember:** `Make_` declares a Tag; `Find_` reads; "build" is only the walk's result.

## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

Decree 0009: a Character's features are its Tags. Each Tag declares its `ENTRIES` (prose) and `CHIPS` (rail values) as plain class data, and `Find_Build( char )` reads them all, now. Julio (2026-09-24): *"start porting."*

The sheet today reads a different source: the `Feature` objects that `FeaturesKit.grant` stores on `char.features`, serialised into the sheet's data and printed by `app/components/character_sheet.py`. Moving the sheet onto the walk is the port.

## 🛠️ What landed (commit `a4beaeb`)

`AtlasActorLudi/Charts_of_Build.py`, in four small steps:

1. `Declaring_Tags( char )`: each carried leaf (`TopKit.Tags`, application order), then its Form (`TopKit.Form`, Base first), each Tag once.
2. `Own_Chips( tag )`: only what the Tag itself declared (`vars( tag )["CHIPS"]`), never inherited.
3. `Read_Chip`: a function value is called with the Character now. A Chip that fails raises `Build_Read_Error` naming the Tag and the label.
4. `Find_Build( char ) -> Build( chips=… )`.

The one Tag it reads today is Unarmored Defense (QST-0093.12). Across one Character per Guild at levels 1, 5 and 20 (39 Characters) it read 6 Chips and raised nothing. **Nothing prints from the walk yet.**

The Chip shape is `AtlasVenustas.Chip( symbol, label, value )`, the one QST-0141 recommends. No new shape was added.

## ❓ Questions to answer before the next slice

1. **What does an Entry declaration look like?** Decree 0009 says `ENTRIES`, and `AtlasVenustas.Entry` already exists as a *printed* entry (a string). A declaration needs at least a title, a text (plain or a function of the Character), a section and the level it is gained at. Reuse `AtlasVenustas.Entry` with a function-valued text, as Chips now do? Or a small declaration record in `Compass_of_Features` that the sheet turns into an `AtlasVenustas.Entry`? My recommendation is the second: it keeps the character layer free of HTML.
2. **Which section order?** Dialog 0026 R3 proposed Species, Background, Guild, Feats, Proficiencies, Equipment, Backstory, Magic, Others. It was proposed, never confirmed. Confirm it, or reorder it.
3. **Where does the level come from?** The layout orders entries by the level they were gained at (Decree 0009, consequences). A Training Tag knows its `MIN_LEVEL`. A Tag like Unarmored Defense is gained at a different level per source (Barbarian 1, Dance 3). Should the Tag record the level it was applied at, or should prose stay with the source Tags, as it does now?
4. **Which family goes first on the sheet?** The rail is the smallest step. The walk's Chips could join the rail next to the Feature Chips, and each ported Tag's Chip would leave its old Training declaration in the same commit. Or port one whole family, Chips and Entries together (Fighter's Second Wind was the Dialog 0026 example).
5. **The sheet's input.** The web sheet prints from serialised data, not from the Character. Should the build be serialised with the rest (`Build` into the sheet's data), or should the sheet layer call `Find_Build` itself before serialising?

## 🎯 Desired outcome

The sheet prints the build, family by family, and `char.features` shrinks until it can be retired (Decree 0009, consequences).

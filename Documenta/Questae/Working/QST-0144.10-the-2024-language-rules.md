## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

Researched from the 2024 Player's Handbook chapter 2 (Choose Languages, the Standard and Rare tables), the SRD 5.2.1 text and the class chapter, then compared with `AtlasLudus/Map_of_Languages.py` and every place that grants a player a language. Three readers (rules, program, comparison), 2026-10-06; the rules side is high-confidence on every point used below.

**Where the program agrees with 2024:**
- Common is always known: Character_Languages adds 'Common' unconditionally (/home/user/GenLegend/AtlasLudus/Map_of_Languages.py:1380), matching the 2024 'Common plus two' baseline.
- Species grant no languages, as in 2024. No species trait anywhere in /home/user/GenLegend/AtlasActorLudi/SpeciesKit/** or /home/user/GenLegend/AtlasLusoris/Map_of_Species.py touches languages (grep 'languag\|druidic\|cant\|sign language' returns nothing), and the 2014-style species branch at Map_of_Languages.py:1383-1392 is dead (Character at /home/user/GenLegend/AtlasActorLudi/CharactersKit.py:129 defines no __eq__, so `char == "Elf"` is always False). The agreement is accidental, but the observable behaviour matches.
- Backgrounds grant no languages directly, as in 2024: /home/user/GenLegend/AtlasLusoris/BackgroundKit.py only mentions 'language' in flavour prose (lines 1485, 1609, 1616); the Background dataclass has no language field.

- Rogue's extra language may come from either table: Rogue.py:50-51 call AddAnyLanguage() with the default pool all_languages (standard + exotic), consistent with 2024's 'from the language tables' (plural), except that Druidic and Thieves' Cant are absent from the pool (see disagreements).
- Ranger Deft Explorer text says 'learn 2 extra languages of your choice' (/home/user/GenLegend/AtlasLusoris/Map_of_Classes/Training/Ranger.py:54) and the Training entry falls back to 'two languages' (/home/user/GenLegend/AtlasLusoris/AtlasOfTraining/Map_of_Ranger_Training.py:418-436): the described count matches 2024 (mechanics do not, see disagreements).

- Languages are uniform (no speak/read/write split): Linguistics stores a plain set of names and the sheet prints one alphabetical list (Map_of_Languages.py:1340-1349; /home/user/GenLegend/app/components/character_sheet.py:1510). Consistent with 2024's 'knowledge of a language means you can communicate in it, read it, and write it'.
- The exotic/rare pool in code (Abyssal, Celestial, Deep Speech, Infernal, Primordial, Sylvan, Undercommon, plus the misplaced Draconic) overlaps the 2024 Rare table on seven of nine entries (Map_of_Languages.py:1286-1295).
- No subclass or invocation in the program grants a language mechanically; the only subclass text that mentions one is the homebrew Fighter 'Knightly Envoy' Polyglot (/home/user/GenLegend/AtlasLusoris/AtlasOfTraining/Map_of_Fighter_Training.py:1216), which is description-only. Consistent with 2024 having no subclass language grants.
- Monk Tongue of Sun and Moon is descriptive only and does not add a language (/home/user/GenLegend/AtlasLusoris/Map_of_Classes/Training/Monk.py:157), so the program agrees that it is comprehension rather than a known language (though the feature itself should not exist in 2024, see disagreements).

**Where it disagrees** (the summary): The GenLegend player language path agrees with 2024 on the skeleton (Common always known; species, backgrounds and PHB feats grant nothing; Rogue gets Thieves' Cant; languages are uniform) but disagrees on nearly every number and mechanism. Verified in the files: (1) the creation step grants 1 standard language instead of 2 (Map_of_Languages.py:1401); (2) the Standard/Rare split is the 2014 one, with Draconic in exotic and Druidic/Thieves' Cant missing from every table (:1286-1295); (3) random picks use set.pop() hash order, not the seeded Dice_Bag streams, so the same seed gives different languages in different processes, and there is no d12 weighting (:1327-1337; choose_new_languages at :1363 is dead and raises TypeError on a set); (4) Rogue gets 2 extra languages instead of 1 (Training/Rogue.py:49-51); (5) Druid never learns Druidic and Ranger never gets Deft Explorer's two languages because the Training Tags are description-only and the Character_Languages class/species branches compare a Character object to strings (Character has no __eq__), leaving them dead; (6) background-carried setting feats Agitator and Dragon Cult Initiate lose their language because Grimoire_of_Characters.py:213 replaces char.languages after Apply_Background_Training ran at :198; (7) Dark Gifts Echoing Soul and Symbiotic Being import a nonexistent STANDARD_LANGUAGES and crash the summon (Map_of_Official_Origin_Feats.py:1598, 1991); plus 2014 leftovers in Thieves' Cant/Druidic text and Monk Tongue of Sun and Moon, and a missing Linguistics.names() that makes spellbooks always say 'Common'. Proposed fixes: redefine the tables to 2024, draw two standard languages via a seeded weighted d12 (re-rolling duplicates) through a Dice_Bag iterating sorted pools, give the Druidic/Thieves' Cant/Deft Explorer Training Tags real apply= grants that also write char.language_feature_grants, make Character_Languages fold in pre-existing grants instead of overwriting, fix the Dark Gift imports and seed their picks, and delete the dead species/class branches.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

A level-1 Character knows Common plus two Standard languages, rolled on the printed d12 table from the Character's own Dice Bag (`identity.languages`), distinct and seed-stable; the tables are the 2024 ones (Draconic standard; Druidic and Thieves' Cant rare and listed); a Rogue knows Thieves' Cant plus one language from either table (`Rogue.languages`), a Druid knows Druidic, a Ranger at level 2 two languages of either table (`Deft_Explorer.languages`), each granted by its own lesson Tag and named on the sheet; a language granted by an Origin Feat during the background step survives to the sheet; the dead species branch, the `set.pop` draw and the 2014 texts are gone; `verify_dice_purposes` sees the language purposes and no hash-ordered pick.

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer

- The `set.pop()` draw is why the sheet gate needed `PYTHONHASHSEED=0`: the language a seed got depended on the process. After this, it does not.
- The two Dark Gifts' crash (a name that does not exist) is fixed in station 5 pass 1 (group 4); this questa only moves their draw to the standard table and the feat's own bag.
- Setting feats outside the PHB (Agitator, Dragon Cult Initiate, the Dark Gifts) keep their language grants on purpose (Julio's call, recorded in the Map); the fix here is that the grant reaches the sheet.

## ✅ Julio's rulings (2026-10-06, in chat)

*"Fix the languages following the 2024 rules, and move draconic to its right place, but the choices should be weighted by species as they have also cultural importance (Elfs elven, Dragonborn draconic....). Secret tongues stay in free picks, yeah, it's more interesting this way. rogues know Thieves' Cant by default anyway, right? Remove the monk's thingy."*

1. **The two creation picks are weighted by the book's d12 and by the Character's species.** The printed faces are the base (Dwarvish, Elvish and Halfling two faces each, the rest one). The species' own tongue, when it is on the Standard table, adds `SPECIES_TONGUE_FACES = 6` faces (half a die): an Elf's first pick is Elvish more often than not, and the second pick is the plain d12 among the rest. Each Species declares its tongue as plain class data, `TONGUE = "Elvish"`, in its own file (Dwarf Dwarvish, Elf Elvish, Dragonborn Draconic, Gnome Gnomish, Goliath Giant, Halfling Halfling, Orc Orc; Human none; Tiefling Infernal and Aasimar Celestial are Rare tongues, so they add no faces at creation and wait for a later ruling on Rare picks). Affinity is a nudge, not a filter (Decree 0005): every standard language stays possible.
2. **Druidic and Thieves' Cant stay in the free picks** (rules as written). A Rogue knows Thieves' Cant at level 1 by default and picks one more language from either table; the pick never lands on a language already known.
3. **The Monk's Tongue of the Sun and Moon is removed** (2024: Deflect Energy stands at level 13).

## ❓ House-rule questions for Julio (asked in chat, 2026-10-06; answered above)

1. **The d12 weights.** The printed table rolls 1d12 with Dwarvish, Elvish and Halfling on two faces each. Use the printed weights (recommended, it is the book) or an even draw?
2. **Druidic and Thieves' Cant in the "any language" picks.** By the book a Rogue's or Ranger's free pick can land on them (they are on the Rare table). Allow it (recommended, rules as written) or keep the two secret tongues out of free picks?
3. **The Monk's Tongue of the Sun and Moon** at level 13 is a 2014 feature the 2024 book removed (Deflect Energy stands there). Remove it (recommended) or keep it as a house rule?

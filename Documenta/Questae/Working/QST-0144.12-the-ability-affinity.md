## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

The program holds four opinions about which abilities a Character favours, and they do not agree:

- `AtlasLusoris/Grimoire_of_Guilds.py`: the Records `PRIMARY`, `SECONDARY` and `ABILITY_PREFERENCE` on every Guild (lines 585 to 640), the `_LEANING_BY_ABILITY` table (line 310) that adds a Strong/Dexterous/Hardy/Clever/Wise/Charming leaning Tag as a Base of each Guild, the `primary_ability` Action (line 1375, no caller), and `_ability_opinions` / `ability_weights` / `ability_preference` (lines 2709 to 2990), which read the preference and the opinions of the Tags the Character holds.
- `AtlasLusoris/Grimoire_of_Characters.py:669`: the rolled scores are placed by `ability_weights`.
- `AtlasLusoris/FeatKit.py:_raise_one_of`: an improvement raises the first ability of `ability_preference`, odd scores first (so the modifier moves), ties broken by the feat's Dice Bag.
- The selectors of skills, weapons, feats and spells read none of it: each has its own guards (`if "Wizard" in char`, `char.subclass == ...`) and draws uniformly from what the guards left.

Nothing live reads the leanings; PRIMARY and SECONDARY are read only to build `ABILITY_PREFERENCE`. Julio's words (Dialog 0027, round eight): *"It's soooo not elegant code."*

## 🧾 Evidence

- `grep -rn "Strong\|Dexterous\|Hardy\|Clever\|Wise\|Charming" --include=*.py` outside `Grimoire_of_Guilds.py`: no reader of a leaning.
- `ability_weights` is called from `Grimoire_of_Characters.py:669` (placement) and twice inside `Grimoire_of_Guilds.py` (its own views); `ability_preference` from `FeatKit._raise_one_of` and `BackgroundKit` (the Background's boost tie-break).
- Station 5 (QST-0144.6) made every draw a Tag-named Dice Bag, so a weight can now be applied at the draw without moving any seed twice: `Pick( ledger, weights, dice=... )` already takes weights.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

Julio's design, read back in plain words:

1. **One vector, on the scores record.** The ability-scores object (`Grimoire_of_AbilityScores.AbilityScores`, carried by every Character) holds an *affinity*: six numbers, one per ability, starting at zero.
2. **Every Tag that cares adds to it,** 0 to +3 per ability, when it is applied: a Guild (the only Tag allowed +3, and on one ability only), a Specialization, a Background, a Feature, a Feat. A Species adds nothing (the 2024 rules give no species ability bonus). The agent proposes the small values as a convention: Guild +3 on its main ability and +1 or +2 on a second; Background +1 on each of its three abilities; a Feature or Feat +1 on what it rewards; so a stack of small Tags cannot outweigh the class.
3. **The affinity weights; it never gates.** Where a selector has a choice left after its gates, the weight of an option is a function of the affinity of the abilities it serves (plus one, so no option has weight zero), handed to `Pick( ledger, weights, dice=char.Dice_Bag( "<Tag>.<choice>" ) )`. The Dice Bag keeps it varied.
4. **The gates filter.** A `@Pre` on the Tag (or the plain condition in a helper of the Tag's file) says what is allowed: a wand for a caster, Heavy Armor Master only with heavy armour proficiency, a spell only from the class list at a level the slots allow. "Stupid choices" never reach the draw; the affinity never lets one through.
5. **Where the affinity is read.** (a) Placement of the rolled scores at creation: highest roll to the highest affinity, ties broken by the `identity.scores` bag. (b) Ability Score Improvements and the feats that carry a raise: the ability with the highest affinity among those under the cap, odd scores first (an odd score rounds up to the next modifier), ties by the feat's bag. (c) Skills, tool and weapon picks, Weapon Masteries, fighting styles, invocations, spells: weights as in 3.
6. **Multiclass reads the same vector**: a second Guild adds its own +3, and the sum is the Character's preference; no PRIMARY of the "main" class.
7. **What goes:** `PRIMARY`, `SECONDARY`, `ABILITY_PREFERENCE`, `_LEANING_BY_ABILITY` and the six leaning Tags, `primary_ability`, `_ability_opinions`; `ability_weights` and `ability_preference` become views of the affinity (the names may stay, the bodies read the vector).

## 🛠️ The plan (one branch, three commits, after station 5)

1. **The record.** `AbilityScores` gains the affinity (six ints, zero) with `Add_Affinity( ability, points )` guarded (0 to 3; a Guild's +3 at most once per Guild) and `Affinity_Weight( abilities ) -> int`; its self-test proves the guard and the view. Every Guild, Specialization, Background and Feat that today declares a preference adds its points in its Imprint (one line per Tag, read from the Tag's own class data, no table in another file). The old Records and the leanings are deleted. Gate: identical fingerprints (the placement reads the same order the old preference gave; where it does not, declared per Guild).
2. **The readers.** Placement, the raises, and the Background boost read the affinity. Gate: identical, or declared where the old tie-break order and the new one disagree.
3. **The weights.** Each selector that has a Tag-named bag hands weights derived from the affinity; the gates stay as they are (station 11 cleans them). Gate: declared (every weighted draw moves once).

## ❓ Questions for Julio (asked in chat, 2026-10-07)

1. **The values.** Guild +3 on one ability and +1 or +2 on a second; Background +1 on its three; Feature or Feat +1; Species 0. Agree, or do you want the Guild to give +3 alone?
2. **The weight function.** For an option serving abilities A, weight = 1 + sum of affinity(a) for a in A? Or the maximum instead of the sum? The sum favours options that serve two favoured abilities; the maximum keeps a one-ability option as strong.
3. **Placement.** Highest roll to highest affinity, strictly, ties by the bag? Or a weighted draw, so a Fighter sometimes places the 15 on Constitution?
4. **The raises.** Highest affinity under the cap, odd first; and the +2 of an ASI as two +1 on the same ability unless it is capped?

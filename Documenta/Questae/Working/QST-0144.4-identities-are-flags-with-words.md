## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

TopKit's rule: only a Flag answers to a word, and exactly. GenLegend's identity Tags were Flags only in part: Role, Gender and Kinship were, Species, Heritage, Guild, Specialization, Background and Alignment were not. So titles, stories, materials and gear asked for a Character's identity by comparing strings (`char.species == "Goliath"`, `char_class == "Paladin"`, `_bears( char, "Wizard" )`): 15 to 44 files name a Species or a Guild outside its kit (Dialog 0027, Evidence §2). A rule written as data could not ask the Character the rulebook's word.

Julio's ruling (Dialog 0027, ruling 6, 2026-10-05): *"lasting identities should become flags to use in spaces like the names and titles. Also gender, alignment... those can mark to set titles and backstory. 'Effects' like dizzy or poisoned should not be tags, but records. Identity is tags (ser/estar in the guide)."*

## 🧾 Evidence

- Probe on 0.2.0a4 (Dialog 0027, Evidence §4): `Flag( "Martial Arts" )( tag )` on a factory-made Tag, applied after other Tags, answers `"Martial Arts" in char`; the class name answers too.
- No Tag in the tree declares `__contains__` or `__iter__`, so a Flag collides with nothing (the two definitions found belong to a dict subclass and a helper class).
- On a generated Elf Wizard Sage after the change: `"Elf"`, `"Dark Elf"`, `"Wizard"`, `"Bladesinger"`, `"Sage"`, `"Player"` in char are True; `"Fighter"`, `"Orc"` False; a True Neutral Character answers False to all four alignment words, because neutral is the absence of a Tag (AlignmentKit's own test).

## 🎯 Desired outcome

Every identity a title, a story or a rule may ask about is a Flag whose word is its display name. Passing conditions stay Records. Then, family by family, the readers that compare strings ask the word instead, and the maps keyed by a name are measured down to zero (QST-0091).

## 🛠️ What landed (2026-10-05, PR #102): the marking

- **Species and Heritages** (28 class statements): `@Flag( "High Elf" )`, the class name with underscores as spaces. Giant Heritages and Celestial Revelations wait for the Species port, where they are features rather than identities.
- **Alignment**: the root and `Good`, `Evil`, `Lawful`, `Chaotic` are Flags; neutral stays the absence of a Tag.
- **Guilds, Specializations, Backgrounds**: `Make_Guild`, `Make_Specialization` and `Make_Background` flag the Tag they make with its name as the word (`"Eldritch Knight" in char`).
- **Gender**: `Male`, `Female` and `Agender` were Flags already; they now carry their pronouns as words (`"He"`, `"Him"`; `"She"`, `"Her"`; `"They"`, `"Them"`), because the story and title tables ask for exactly those words. Kinship and Role were already Flags; unchanged.
- Self-tests: GuildKit, BackgroundKit, AlignmentKit and SpeciesKit each assert one word (`"Rogue" in char`, `"Soldier" in char`, `"Good" in character`, `"Orc" in character`).

## ⚠️ Declared difference: the words wake dormant rules

This station was planned as "identical". It is not, and the reason is worth recording.

GenLegend's older code already asks a Character for a word: `if "Wizard" in char`, `IsStrong = "Barbarian" in char or "Fighter" in char …` (`Grimoire_of_Features`, 19 probes), the hit-dice ladder (`Grimoire_of_Health`, 12), and above all the story engine: `AtlasEpica/Charts_of_The_Monomyth.If` tests every row of the story tables with `conds in host`, and `AtlasEpica/Map_of_Stories` holds **1347 rows conditioned on a Species or a Guild word** and 3 on a pronoun. Those probes were written for TagKit 0.1, which answered any Tag by name. Since the 0.2 move only a Flag answers `in`, so every one of them has been silently False: stories came from the unconditional rows only, a Fighter could never reach "Increased Strength", class-specific Epic Boons were unreachable. `Grimoire_of_Spellcasters._bears` records the same discovery for spells ("every caster lost its spells") and was patched to read `char_class` first; the rest was not.

With the words in place the probes answer again, as their authors wrote them. Measured on the default grid (52 Players, 20 light NonPlayers): **1 of 52 fingerprints** moves (a level-5 Fighter's feat pick, `Increased Constitution` to `Increased Scores`, because `IsStrong` is true again) and **60 of 72 sheets** read differently, all in their story paragraphs: a Bard's academy is now a "Sage Institution" rather than a "Bard Institution" when the Background row wins the draw, and the dice that the new branches consume move the place names after them. On the wide grid (levels 1, 3, 5, 9, 13, 17, 20; seeds 1 to 3): **5 of 273 fingerprints** move, all one Character line (the seed-2 Eldritch Knight Fighter at levels 5, 9, 13, 17 and 20), and only in `features`: its legacy feat picks reshuffle (`Increased Constitution`, `Increased Scores`, `Increased Dexterity` change places; at level 20 `Boon of Speed` becomes `Boon of Fate`) because `IsStrong` and the Fighter boon branch answer again. **294 of 343 sheets** read differently, every difference in the story paragraph. Equipment output identical; replay exact; wide sweep 1164 of 1164; the same two pre-existing self-test failures as on `main`.

This is the behaviour ruling 6 asked for ("flags to use in spaces like the names and titles"), returning. It is a declared difference for Julio to accept or send back; nothing merges before his word. If he prefers the old sheets, the one-line alternative is to keep the words and port the story engine's `If` to ask membership explicitly, which is the readers' station anyway.

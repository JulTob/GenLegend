## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

Every family of Tags made by a factory kept a hand-written list or dictionary beside its declarations, and the catalogue was read from that second copy: `_TRAINING_DECLARATIONS` and `TRAININGS`, `_GUILD_DECLARATIONS` and `GUILDS`, `_SPECIALIZATION_TAGS_BY_GUILD`, three Feat lists, `_INVOCATION_DECLARATIONS`, `_CRAFT_DECLARATIONS`, `_ORDER_TAGS`, `_MANEUVER_TAGS` and `_MASTERY_TAGS` (that last one declared twice in one file, a recovery duplicate). Eleven registries, measured in Dialog 0027. A list beside a declaration drifts (QST-0091's Ranger list already had), and a factory that writes constants into a class namespace has no place to validate them.

Species shows the shape that needs no list: a `@Pin` whose `@Record`s take keyword-only inputs, validate them, and land on the pinned Tag as Reports; its Field, `Available[:]`, is the catalogue, in declaration order.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

Each family declares a Pin; its factory builds the Tag with only its behaviour and pins it; the Pin's Field is the only catalogue; accessor functions keep their names and signatures and read the Field; no Character and no sheet changes; every module self-tests the Field against what the old registry held.

**How it was done.** Eight implementer agents, one per family, each in a worktree made from the branch tip, each with the same written laws (the Species reference, names stay names, factories keep their tables, a `__main__` self-test, the default-grid fingerprint identical before and after, `import app.main`, replay). Behind each, an independent reviewer tried to refute the commit against those laws. **What the reviewers found (2026-10-05).** Seven of eight commits were judged sound on the first try; family E was judged defective on one point only, two explanatory comments written with a plain `#` instead of the `#--` pin, fixed when the commit was picked. Notes that are not failures but belong to the record:

- A refused declaration now raises TopKit's `TagCompositionError` from the Pin's Record instead of the factory's own `ValueError` (families A, B, E). No caller read those messages; a refused Tag never joins the Field.
- The Pins are stricter where the brief asked them to be: `MIN_LEVEL` must be an `int` of at least 1 (a `2.0` or a `True` is refused now), a Craft `tier=True` is refused. Every declaration in the Maps already passed; the catalogues compare equal, order and every constant.
- `Guild.SPECIALIZATIONS` is a live descriptor on the root. Read on a Guild it answers that Guild's Shapes; read on a Shape or on the root it answers nothing, where the old tuple was inherited by every Shape. Its two readers (`WarlockKit`, `FighterKit`) read it on a Guild and answer the same.
- A Field holds its Tags weakly. Every Map holds its minted Tags by module name, so the catalogues are stable; the two families that mint on demand (`order_tag`, `_mastery_tag`) keep a Tag alive through the Character that carries it and mint an equal one afresh otherwise, which no reader can tell apart. The self-tests of A and F assert the Field after a `gc.collect()`.
- Families B and G each wrote a read-only live Mapping over a Pin Field keyed by NAME. One `Field_Index` now lives beside `Report_Of` in `CharactersKit`, and `GUILDS`, `SPECIALIZATIONS_BY_GUILD` and `MANEUVERS` are views of it (the ninth commit).

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer

- A Pin Record may hold a Tag class (`PATH`, `ORIGIN_FEAT`): it lands as a Report on the Tag, never as an Action on the Character. That is why `Report_Of` is unnecessary once a family pins.
- `GUILDS` keeps its name because thirteen files read it; it is a live read-only view of the Field by name, not a second list.
- Names stay names in this station (`GUILD_NAME` is still a string). Turning names into Tags is the readers' work of QST-0144.4 and QST-0091.
- Two pre-existing self-test failures are untouched: `InvocationKit` (`char in "a string"`, QST-0082.1) and `AtlasOfGuilds.FighterKit` (imports itself twice).

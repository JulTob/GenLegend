## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

**Two trees.** The live one, `AtlasActorLudi/AtlasAlusoris/`, is what `app/main.py` summons (`summon_nonplayer`, `summon_nonplayer_list`, `nonplayer_choices`) and what `app/components/npc_sheet.py` renders: eleven modules, every one a 2026-08-29 bytecode recovery (QST-0134) with its defects kept on purpose. The old one, `AtlasAlusoris/`, is never summoned by the app, yet is loaded on every start: fourteen live files import it, and the first is the languages map (`AtlasLudus/Map_of_Languages.py:4`), a player-path file depending on the old monster tree. Its `class NPC` reseeds the shared stream 47 times and its constructor's default seed is drawn at import.

**What a non-player is today.** Already a `Character` (the skeleton: seed, level, dices, Dice Bag) wearing the `NonPlayer` Role Flag and an Alusoris Shape, `Grimoire_of_NPC.NonPlayer`, whose body is a bag of about thirty legacy procedures bound as Actions (SetGender, SetSize, SetTitle, SetSubrace, Naming, SetAC, SetHitPoints, SetMovement, SimpleAttack, Magic, SetMyStory, ...). Its identity already goes through the shared Tags (a Species or a bare Creature Type through `RaceKit`, the Guild, the Background with its Origin Feat, Gender, Alignment, three to five minted Tactical Feature Tags) and the words answer (`"Rogue" in npc`, `"They" in npc`). Everything else is a string or a number written by a procedure: race, subrace, creature_type, genus, name, title, hit points, armour class, saves, skills, senses, resistances, movement, attacks, spells, personality.

**The rite today, against the player's.** The door pre-picks race, guild and background on bags of its own (`identity.nonplayer.*`), so the Tags' own bags never open; alignment comes after the background instead of first; a legacy `SetGender` runs before the Gender Tag and always lands on "They"; no Specialization; no `Resolve_Species_Features`, no background abilities or training on the light path; ability scores from the shared stream between Guild and Background instead of from `AbilityScoresKit` after; no gear, armour class from legacy tables; spells from a string engine; the story drawn at render; five attempts with seed plus one after any exception, where the player attempts once.

**It does not summon in full.** `summon_nonplayer( level=1, seed=1 )` fails on `main` and on the branch, "Unable to summon a NonPlayer Character after five attempts": `SetSize` calls `Map_of_Size.Size( Type )` with two arguments (defect 1 of QST-0134). The light build (`light=True`) works and is the only one the sheet gate and the Scenes engine use; the app's own non-player pages call the door without `light=True` and without a guild, so every page summon fails today and shows the error. The sheet gate pins both shared streams before each summon and turns an exception into one line of text.

**Counts** (verified by the readers with the interpreter): 27 race labels (13 resolve to a Species Shape, 13 to a bare Creature Type, Vampire special-cased); 17 known Species (10 playable, 7 legacy non-player shells with no size or speed); 439 subrace entries in 27 string pickers (an Elf or Gnome non-player carries a Heritage Tag and a contradicting subrace string); 42 retired archetypes decoded into 12 Guilds and 30 Backgrounds; 72 non-player Backgrounds (46 shared, 26 non-player only); 17 tactical feature specs minted into Tags; 26 live draw sites, of which the shared stream is still reached by personality (Trait, Ideal, Plot Hook), senses, region effects, spells, the recharge wording (at import) and the name reseed; 13 modules read `.archetype`; 34 sheet fields, half of them missing on a light non-player and rendered as "-".

## 🎯 Desired outcome

A non-player is built the way a player is: the skeleton, then Tags applied in the player's order, each drawing from its own bag named by the Tag; races, lineages, archetype-derived roles and tactical features are Tags on Pin Fields with Flag words; the numbers (scores, hit points, armour class, saves, skills, size, speed, movement, attacks, spells) come from the player's kits or from Records on the Lineage; the sheet reads Tags and Chips; the old tree is deleted; the non-player grid summons in full on every level and seed, is fingerprinted, and its sheet snapshot is held identical by every station that does not declare otherwise.
